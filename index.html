✨ My Favourite Memory ✨

If there's one memory that always makes me smile, it's from one of my vacations in Gurgaon. What started as a completely normal evening somehow turned into one of the funniest memories I'll ever have. We picked up that electric mosquito racket and suddenly became absolute mosquito hunters. Every mosquito was treated like it was on a wanted list, and every successful hit felt like we'd completed some secret mission. We laughed so much over something so unbelievably random. Looking back now, it wasn't really about the mosquitoes at all—it was about spending time together, joking around, and turning the simplest moment into one I'll never forget. Even today, whenever I see one of those mosquito rackets, I immediately think of that evening and smile. Some memories don't become special because they're grand or extraordinary. They become special because of the people you share them with. And this one will always stay with me.
